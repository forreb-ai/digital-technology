Year 11 Digital Technology

Human Computer Interfaces • Programming • Digital Outcomes

Course Overview

Year 11 Digital Technology focuses on three key areas: Human Computer Interfaces, Creating a Programme using Code, and a Student-Led Project. Throughout the year, students will develop computational thinking, problem-solving, design skills and digital literacy while creating authentic digital outcomes.

Term 1

Human Computer Interfaces

Investigate how people interact with digital technologies and analyse what makes an interface effective. Students will evaluate existing digital products and develop their own interface designs.

Term 2

Create a Programme Using Code

Develop programming skills through the creation of a functioning software solution. Students will apply computational thinking and use variables, selection, iteration and testing processes.

Terms 3 & 4

Student-Led Project

Students select a project pathway and develop a digital outcome based on their interests. Projects follow the technological design process from initial research through to evaluation.

Student-Led Project Pathways

Students choose one of the following pathways to develop into a major digital outcome during Terms 3 and 4.

Website Design

Design and develop a professional website using HTML and CSS. Focus on visual design, usability, navigation and user experience.
